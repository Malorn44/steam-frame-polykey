# Theme credits

The bundled themes: upstream, licence and copyright. Colours are taken from each project's published palette; any blend or pick of our own is noted. Shadows are ours unless noted, since no palette defines them.

- **Catppuccin Mocha** (`catppuccin-mocha.json`): [Catppuccin](https://github.com/catppuccin/palette), MIT, © 2021 Catppuccin.
- **Catppuccin Latte** (`catppuccin-latte.json`): [Catppuccin](https://github.com/catppuccin/palette), MIT, © 2021 Catppuccin.
- **Solarized Dark** (`solarized-dark.json`): [Solarized](https://github.com/altercation/solarized) by Ethan Schoonover, MIT, © 2011 Ethan Schoonover. Modifier, hover and divider are blends of base02 and base01.
- **Solarized Light** (`solarized-light.json`): [Solarized](https://github.com/altercation/solarized) by Ethan Schoonover, MIT, © 2011 Ethan Schoonover. Modifier, hover and divider are blends of base2 and base1.
- **Gruvbox Dark** (`gruvbox-dark.json`): [gruvbox](https://github.com/morhetz/gruvbox) by Pavel Pertsev (morhetz), MIT/X11. Errors are bright_red, as Fn is aqua.
- **Gruvbox Light** (`gruvbox-light.json`): [gruvbox](https://github.com/morhetz/gruvbox) by Pavel Pertsev (morhetz), MIT/X11. Errors are faded_red, as Fn is blue.
- **Nord** (`nord.json`): [Nord](https://github.com/nordtheme/nord) by Sven Greb, MIT, © 2016-present Sven Greb.
- **Nord Light** (`nord-light.json`): [Nord](https://github.com/nordtheme/nord) by Sven Greb, MIT, © 2016-present Sven Greb; Nord's "bright ambiance" use of the palette (Snow Storm surfaces, Polar Night text). Modifier, hover and divider are blends of nord4 and nord3.
- **Tokyo Night** (`tokyo-night.json`): [Tokyo Night](https://github.com/tokyo-night/tokyo-night-vscode-theme) by Enkia, MIT, © 2018-present Enkia. Key is the Storm editor background (#24283b) and modifier and divider Terminal Black (#414868), both from the README's palette; the Night theme's own shades sit too close to its background.
- **Tokyo Night Light** (`tokyo-night-light.json`): [Tokyo Night](https://github.com/tokyo-night/tokyo-night-vscode-theme) by Enkia, MIT, © 2018-present Enkia. Hover is a blend of the UI grey #c1c2c7 and the foreground.
- **Rosé Pine** (`rose-pine.json`): [Rosé Pine](https://github.com/rose-pine/rose-pine-palette), MIT, © mvllow. Errors are love, as Fn is foam.
- **Rosé Pine Dawn** (`rose-pine-dawn.json`): [Rosé Pine](https://github.com/rose-pine/rose-pine-palette), MIT, © mvllow.
- **GitHub Light** (`github-light.json`): GitHub's [Primer primitives](https://github.com/primer/primitives) (light), MIT, © 2018 GitHub Inc. The shadow is Primer's shadow-floating-small.
- **GitHub Dark** (`github-dark.json`): GitHub's [Primer primitives](https://github.com/primer/primitives) (dark), MIT, © 2018 GitHub Inc. The shadow is Primer's shadow-floating-small.
- **Dracula** (`dracula.json`): [Dracula](https://github.com/dracula/dracula-theme) by Zeno Rocha, with the UI shades of [Dracula for VS Code](https://github.com/dracula/visual-studio-code), MIT, © 2016 Dracula Theme. The legend grey (#c0c4d6) is our own, between FG and Comment: Dracula has no grey that reads as a small legend.
- **Night Owl** (`night-owl.json`): [Night Owl](https://github.com/sdras/night-owl-vscode-theme) by Sarah Drasner, MIT, © 2018 Sarah Drasner.
- **Steam** (`steam.json`): Steam's own colours. Surface, keys, letters and modifiers are read from Steam's keyboard while it runs; the colours in the file are the fallbacks, and those and the legend, hover and accent come from the Steam client's UI stylesheet. Fn shares the accent, as Steam's UI has no second accent colour.
