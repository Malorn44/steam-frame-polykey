# Theme credits

The bundled themes after editor colour schemes: upstream, licence and copyright. Colours are taken from each project's published palette; any blend or pick of our own is noted. Shadows are ours unless noted, since no palette defines them.

- **Catppuccin Mocha** (`catppuccin-mocha.json`): [Catppuccin](https://github.com/catppuccin/palette), MIT, © 2021 Catppuccin.
- **Catppuccin Latte** (`catppuccin-latte.json`): [Catppuccin](https://github.com/catppuccin/palette), MIT, © 2021 Catppuccin.
- **Solarized Dark** (`solarized-dark.json`): [Solarized](https://github.com/altercation/solarized) by Ethan Schoonover, MIT, © 2011 Ethan Schoonover. Modifier, hover and divider are blends of base02 and base01.
- **Solarized Light** (`solarized-light.json`): [Solarized](https://github.com/altercation/solarized) by Ethan Schoonover, MIT, © 2011 Ethan Schoonover. Modifier, hover and divider are blends of base2 and base1.
- **Gruvbox Dark** (`gruvbox-dark.json`): [gruvbox](https://github.com/morhetz/gruvbox) by Pavel Pertsev (morhetz), MIT/X11. Errors are bright_red, as Fn is aqua.
- **Gruvbox Light** (`gruvbox-light.json`): [gruvbox](https://github.com/morhetz/gruvbox) by Pavel Pertsev (morhetz), MIT/X11. Errors are faded_red, as Fn is blue.
- **Nord** (`nord.json`): [Nord](https://github.com/nordtheme/nord) by Sven Greb, MIT, © 2016-present Sven Greb.
- **Nord Light** (`nord-light.json`): [Nord](https://github.com/nordtheme/nord) by Sven Greb, MIT, © 2016-present Sven Greb; Nord's "bright ambiance" use of the palette (Snow Storm surfaces, Polar Night text). Modifier, hover and divider are blends of nord4 and nord3.
- **Tokyo Night** (`tokyo-night.json`): [Tokyo Night](https://github.com/tokyo-night/tokyo-night-vscode-theme) by Enkia, MIT, © 2018-present Enkia. Key is the Storm editor background (#24283b) and modifier and divider Terminal Black (#414868), both from the README's palette; the Night theme's own shades sit too close to its background.
